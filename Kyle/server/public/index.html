2. In ntfy tap +, enter this topic and tap Subscribe: loading…
3. Allow notifications when iOS asks.
4. On iPhone open the Watch app → Notifications → ntfy → Mirror my iPhone.
Alerts appear on the watch when your iPhone is locked and the watch is on your wrist. Keep the topic private: anyone who knows it can read these alerts.
